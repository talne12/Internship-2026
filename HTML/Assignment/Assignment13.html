Big Concert Hall

The Bing concert Hall at Standard will play a vital role on campusand in the localcommunity.The hall's exceptional acoustics and state-of-the-art technical capabilities will showcase the world's finest performers.

The hall is scheduled to open early in 2013.The concert hall will have 844 seats in an intimate vineyard-style configuration sections wrapping all the way around the stage.
